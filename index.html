Free self-help guides

Short, accurate, and written for non-technical users. If a step doesn't work, call us and we'll take it from there.

Guide 1 — You can't sign in to your account

  1. Check that Caps Lock is off and that the email address is spelled exactly as registered.
  2. Use the “Forgot password” link and check both your inbox and spam folder for the reset email (it can take up to 10 minutes).
  3. If you use two-factor authentication, make sure your device's clock is set to automatic — a drifting clock is the most common cause of rejected codes.
  4. Try a different browser or a private/incognito window to rule out cached data.
  5. Still locked out? Call +1 (888) 562-2563 — we'll help you work through the recovery options.

Guide 2 — Your printer won't connect

  1. Confirm the printer and the computer are on the same Wi‑Fi network (a 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz mismatch is a frequent culprit).
  2. Power-cycle in order: printer off → router off for 30 seconds → router on → printer on.
  3. On your computer, remove the printer from your device list, then add it again as a new device.
  4. Download the current driver from the printer manufacturer's official website — not a third-party driver site.
  5. If it still won't appear, call us and we'll diagnose the network side of the problem.

Guide 3 — Email isn't syncing on your phone

  1. Turn off any battery-saver or “data saver” mode, which can block background sync.
  2. Check that the account's sync setting is enabled for Mail in your device settings.
  3. Remove the account and re-add it — you'll need your email password and, if enabled, your app password.
  4. If you recently changed your email password, update it on the device as well; an old password silently blocks sync.
  5. No luck? Call +1 (888) 562-2563 and we'll walk through the server settings with you.

Guide 4 — Preparing a device for backup or transfer

  1. Free up at least 20% of your storage before starting — transfers fail on full drives.
  2. Connect to power and to a stable Wi‑Fi network; avoid mobile hotspots for large transfers.
  3. Write down (or export) your list of installed applications before you move anything.
  4. Run a test restore of a single small folder to confirm the backup actually works.
  5. For a full migration, call us to schedule a guided session at a quoted flat rate.
